## What a Buyback Can Include

- Your down payment and the monthly payments you made
- The remaining loan or lease balance, paid directly to the lender
- Registration, taxes, and certain fees
- Attorney fees and costs, paid by the manufacturer

California law allows the manufacturer to deduct a usage offset based on the miles driven before the first repair for the defect. [How buybacks are calculated](/lemon-law/buyback-calculation/)
