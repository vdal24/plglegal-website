## California Lemon Law Attorney

If your new or leased car keeps going back to the dealer for the same problem, California law can require the manufacturer to buy it back, replace it, or pay you cash. The manufacturer also pays your attorney fees when you win.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to pay a lawyer for a lemon law case?
Usually not. California's lemon law requires the manufacturer to pay the consumer's attorney fees and costs when the consumer prevails.

### Does lemon law cover leased cars?
Yes. Leased vehicles with a manufacturer's new vehicle warranty are covered.

### What documents do I need?
Your purchase or lease contract, your registration, and every repair order from the dealer. If you do not have the repair orders, we can request them.

### How long does a buyback take?
It depends on the manufacturer and the facts. Many claims resolve without a lawsuit, and some require litigation. We keep you updated at every step.
