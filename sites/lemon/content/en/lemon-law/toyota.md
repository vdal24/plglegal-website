## Reliable Brands Still Make Lemons

Toyota and Lexus have a strong reputation, which is exactly why owners hesitate to push back when something goes wrong. California's lemon law does not grade on reputation. If your Tundra, Tacoma, RAV4, Camry, Highlander, Grand Highlander, Sequoia, 4Runner, Prius, bZ4X, or Lexus has a defect the dealer cannot fix, you may be entitled to a buyback or replacement, and Toyota pays your attorney fees if you prevail.

## Toyota and Lexus Problems Owners Commonly Report

Examples of issues that have been reported or subject to recalls:

- **Twin-turbo V6 engine problems** in some Tundra and Lexus LX models, which were the subject of a 2024 recall involving engine failure risk
- **Hybrid system faults:** warning lights, inverter problems, reduced power
- **bZ4X EV issues,** including an early recall involving wheel hub bolts, and charging or range complaints
- **Infotainment and connectivity failures**
- **Transmission hesitation or rough shifting**
- **Water leaks and rattles**

## Do Not Let "It's a Toyota" Talk You Out of Your Rights

Dealers sometimes tell owners a recurring problem is "normal" or "within spec." If the problem substantially affects how your vehicle drives, its value, or its safety, and it keeps coming back, have it reviewed. Make sure each repair order records your complaint accurately.

## 2025 Procedure Changes

California's 2025 lemon law changes created a new pre-suit demand process for manufacturers that opted in, including a 30-day notice before filing and a 30-day window for the manufacturer to make an offer. We determine which rules apply to your Toyota or Lexus.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Toyota has a reputation for reliability. Can my Toyota still be a lemon?
Yes. Reputation does not matter under California law. What matters is whether your specific vehicle has a warranty defect that was not fixed within a reasonable number of repair attempts.

### Does lemon law cover Toyota hybrids?
Yes. Hybrid systems, including hybrid batteries and inverters, are covered by the manufacturer's warranty and can be the basis of a lemon law claim.

### Is Lexus covered too?
Yes. Lexus vehicles sold or leased in California with a new vehicle warranty are covered by the same law.
