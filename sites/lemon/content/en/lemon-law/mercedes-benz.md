## Does Your Mercedes Qualify?

Your vehicle may be a lemon if all of these are true:

- It was sold or leased in California with a **manufacturer's warranty**
- It has a defect covered by that warranty that **substantially affects its use, value, or safety**
- An authorized Mercedes-Benz dealer has had a **reasonable number of chances to fix it** and the problem keeps coming back, or the vehicle has spent **30 or more total days** in the shop

You do not need to be sure. Send us your repair orders and we will tell you where you stand at no charge.

## What a Mercedes-Benz Lemon Law Recovery Can Include

- **Repurchase (buyback):** a refund of your down payment and monthly payments, payoff of your loan or lease, and registration and similar charges, less a mileage offset for the miles you drove before the first repair visit for the defect
- **Replacement:** a new, substantially identical vehicle
- **Cash and keep:** a cash payment while you keep the vehicle, in appropriate cases
- **Incidental costs** such as towing and rental cars
- **Civil penalty:** if the manufacturer willfully violated the law, a court may award up to two times your actual damages
- **Attorney fees and costs paid by the manufacturer** when you prevail

See how the numbers work: [lemon law buyback calculation](/lemon-law/buyback-calculation/). Every case depends on its facts, and no outcome is guaranteed.

## What to Do Right Now

- **Keep every repair order.** Ask for one each visit, even if the dealer says "could not duplicate."
- **Describe the problem the same way each time** and make sure the paperwork matches what you said.
- **Note the days out of service,** including time waiting for parts.
- **Keep using an authorized Mercedes-Benz dealer** for warranty repairs.
- **Do not accept a trade-in offer or sign a release** before you know what a lemon law claim is worth.

## Business-Owned Vehicles and Sprinters

California's lemon law also protects small businesses. A vehicle bought or used primarily for business can be covered if the business has five or fewer vehicles registered in California and the vehicle has a gross vehicle weight under 10,000 pounds. This matters for many Mercedes-Benz and Sprinter owners. Some heavier Sprinter configurations are over the limit, so we check the weight rating first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many repair attempts make a Mercedes-Benz a lemon in California?
There is no fixed number. The manufacturer must fix a warranty defect within a reasonable number of attempts. California presumes a vehicle is a lemon if, within 18 months or 18,000 miles of delivery, it was repaired two or more times for a defect that could cause death or serious injury, four or more times for the same defect, or was out of service for more than 30 days in total. A vehicle can still qualify outside that window.

### What does it cost to hire a Mercedes-Benz lemon law attorney?
For most clients, nothing out of pocket. The Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act requires the manufacturer to pay the consumer's reasonable attorney fees and costs when the consumer prevails. We do not charge for the case review.

### Can I get a buyback on a leased Mercedes?
Yes. Leased vehicles with a new vehicle warranty are covered. A lease repurchase typically refunds the payments you made and pays off the lease, less a mileage offset.

### Are Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans covered by lemon law?
Sprinter vans used for personal or family purposes are covered. Vans bought or used primarily for business can be covered if the business has five or fewer vehicles registered in California and the van has a gross vehicle weight under 10,000 pounds. Some heavier Sprinter configurations exceed that limit, so coverage should be confirmed for your van.

### Does lemon law cover electric Mercedes models like the EQS and EQE?
Yes. Electric vehicles are covered the same way as gas vehicles. Charging failures, high-voltage battery faults, loss of range, and repeated drive system warnings can all support a claim when the dealer cannot fix them in a reasonable number of attempts.

### What if my Mercedes is certified pre-owned or I bought it used?
It depends on the warranty that came with the sale. A vehicle sold with a manufacturer's warranty, such as a Mercedes-Benz Certified Pre-Owned warranty, can be covered. Coverage for other used vehicles is narrower. We review the paperwork for free. See [lemon law for used cars](/lemon-law/used-cars/).

### How long does a Mercedes lemon law case take?
Many cases resolve within a few months of the demand. Cases that go to litigation take longer. Recent California procedures require early document exchange and mediation in many cases, which can speed resolution.

### Do I have to keep driving the car while the case is pending?
Usually you keep the vehicle, keep making payments, and keep it insured and maintained until the case resolves. If the vehicle is unsafe, tell the dealer in writing and talk to us about your options.
