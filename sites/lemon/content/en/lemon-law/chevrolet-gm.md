## Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac Owners

General Motors sells more vehicles in the U.S. than almost anyone, under the Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac brands. When a Silverado, Sierra, Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon, Escalade, Equinox, Traverse, Colorado, Blazer, or one of GM's newer EVs has a defect the dealer cannot fix, California's lemon law gives you real leverage.

If GM cannot repair a warranty defect after a reasonable number of attempts, it may have to buy back the vehicle, replace it, or pay you to keep it, and it must pay your attorney fees if you prevail.

## GM Problems Owners Commonly Report

Examples of issues GM owners have reported or that have been the subject of recalls:

- **6.2L V8 engine problems** in full-size trucks and SUVs, which were the subject of a large 2025 recall
- **8-speed and 10-speed transmission concerns:** shuddering, harsh shifts, hesitation, and torque converter issues
- **EV battery problems,** including the high-voltage battery recall that affected the Chevy Bolt EV and EUV
- **Electrical and infotainment failures:** screens going dark, camera failures, battery drain
- **Air conditioning failures and leaks**
- **Driver assistance and safety system faults**

Your vehicle does not need to be on this list. If a warranty defect keeps coming back, it can qualify.

## GM Recalls and Your Lemon Law Rights

A recall does not automatically make a vehicle a lemon. But a recall repair that does not fix the problem, takes your vehicle out of service for weeks, or has to be repeated can be strong evidence in a lemon law claim. Keep every recall and repair record.

## The 2025 California Lemon Law Changes

California changed its lemon law procedures in 2025. Manufacturers that opted in must receive a written demand at least 30 days before a lawsuit and have 30 days to offer a repurchase or replacement. We confirm which procedure applies to your GM vehicle and handle the demand for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which GM brands are covered by California lemon law?
All of them, including Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac, as long as the vehicle was sold or leased in California with a new vehicle warranty.

### Does a GM recall mean my vehicle is a lemon?
Not automatically. A recall shows a known defect, but lemon law depends on whether the defect substantially impairs your vehicle and was not fixed within a reasonable number of attempts. A recall repair that fails or keeps recurring can support a claim.

### What if GM offers me an extended warranty instead of a buyback?
Talk to a lawyer before you accept. An extended warranty may be worth far less than a repurchase, and some offers ask you to release your claims.
