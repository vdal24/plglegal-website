## California Leads the Country in EVs, and in EV Problems

More electric vehicles are sold in California than anywhere else in the U.S. EVs have fewer moving parts, but they bring new failure points: high-voltage batteries, onboard chargers, charging control units, and software. California's lemon law covers them all.

## EV and Hybrid Defects That Can Qualify

- Vehicle will not charge, or charging stops randomly
- Charging control unit failures (some have been subject to recall)
- Sudden or abnormal range loss
- Reduced power or "turtle mode" warnings
- High-voltage battery faults and battery replacement
- Drive unit noises or failures
- 12-volt battery failures that leave the car dead
- Thermal management and battery heating or cooling problems
- Software problems that disable features

## Common EV Recalls and Complaints

Examples include battery fire-risk recalls affecting the Chevy Bolt and certain Jeep 4xe plug-in hybrids, charging control unit recalls on some Hyundai and Kia EVs, and high-voltage system recalls on some Ford Mustang Mach-E vehicles. Owners of [Tesla](/lemon-law/tesla/), [Ford](/lemon-law/ford/), [GM](/lemon-law/chevrolet-gm/), [Hyundai](/lemon-law/hyundai/), [Kia](/lemon-law/kia/), [Mercedes-Benz](/lemon-law/mercedes-benz/), and Toyota EVs have all reported charging and battery complaints.

## Waiting for Parts Counts

EV repairs often involve long waits for batteries or modules. Every day your vehicle is at the dealer for a warranty repair counts toward the 30-day out-of-service presumption under California law. Keep track of your loaner and rental dates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are electric vehicles covered by California lemon law?
Yes. Electric vehicles, plug-in hybrids, and hybrids are covered the same as gas vehicles when sold or leased in California with a new vehicle warranty.

### Is range loss a lemon law defect?
Abnormal range loss caused by a battery or system defect can be. Normal, gradual battery aging is different. An attorney can review your service records and range data.

### My EV was out of service waiting for parts. Do those days count?
Yes. Days the vehicle is at the dealer for warranty repairs, including waiting for parts, count toward the 30-day out-of-service presumption.
