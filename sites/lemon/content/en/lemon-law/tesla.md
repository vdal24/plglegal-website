## Tesla Owners Are Protected by California Lemon Law

Tesla sells more electric vehicles in California than anyone else, and its direct sales and service model works differently from traditional dealers. That does not change your rights. If your Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, or Cybertruck has a warranty defect that Tesla cannot fix after a reasonable number of attempts, Tesla may have to buy it back, replace it, or pay you, plus your attorney fees.

## Tesla Problems Owners Commonly Report

Examples of issues Tesla owners have reported, some of which have been the subject of federal investigations or recalls:

- **Unexpected braking ("phantom braking")** while using driver assistance features, which was the subject of a federal safety investigation
- **Touchscreen and computer failures,** including the media control unit issue that led to a recall in older Model S and Model X vehicles
- **Suspension and steering problems:** clunking, control arm failures, alignment issues
- **High-voltage battery and charging problems:** range loss, charging errors, reduced power warnings
- **Water leaks,** wind noise, and trunk or frunk seal problems
- **Cybertruck issues** that have been subject to recalls, including the accelerator pedal
- **Camera, sensor, and Autopilot faults** that disable features you paid for

## Tesla Repairs, the App, and Your Paper Trail

With Tesla, much of your repair history lives in the Tesla app and in invoices emailed after service. That record is your evidence. Make sure every service request describes the problem in plain terms, save screenshots, and keep every invoice and estimate. Over-the-air updates meant to address a defect may also count as repair attempts.

## Tesla's Warranty

Tesla vehicles typically come with a basic vehicle limited warranty and a separate, longer warranty for the high-voltage battery and drive unit. Problems that appear during either warranty can support a lemon law claim. We review your specific coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do over-the-air software updates count as repair attempts?
They can. If Tesla pushes an update intended to fix a reported defect, that may count as a repair attempt. Keep screenshots of your service requests and the release notes for updates tied to your problem.

### Can I use California lemon law if Tesla handled my repairs through mobile service?
Yes. Mobile service visits and service center visits both count. Make sure each service request in the app accurately describes the problem.

### Does Tesla have to pay my attorney fees?
If you prevail on a California lemon law claim, the manufacturer must pay your reasonable attorney fees and costs, including Tesla.
