Where Burbank Crashes Happen

  • Olive Avenue, Alameda Avenue, and Riverside Drive near the studio lots, with heavy production and commuter traffic
  • Victory Boulevard and Hollywood Way, around Hollywood Burbank Airport, with rideshare, shuttle, and rental car traffic
  • San Fernando Boulevard and downtown Burbank, with pedestrians, parking, and left-turn collisions
  • The 5, 134, and 170 freeways, where merging and stop-and-go traffic cause rear-end and sideswipe crashes

What to Do After a Burbank Crash

  1. Call 911. Burbank Police generally handle city streets; CHP handles the freeways.
  2. Photograph the vehicles, the scene, and your injuries.
  3. Get the other driver's insurance and, if it is a work vehicle, the company name.
  4. See a doctor promptly.
  5. Do not give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer.
  6. Call us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who handles the police report for a Burbank crash?

Burbank Police Department typically handles crashes on city streets. Crashes on the 5, 134, or 170 freeways are generally handled by the California Highway Patrol.

I was hit by a studio or production vehicle. Who pays?

If the driver was working when the crash happened, the employer or production company and its insurer are often responsible in addition to the driver. We identify every policy that may apply.
