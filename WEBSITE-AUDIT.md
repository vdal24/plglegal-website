# Website Audit: plglegal.com (November 2026)

Nothing was committed, pushed, or deployed. Run `python3 build.py` to rebuild. Before and after home page screenshots are in `audit-shots/`.

## What I found

- The strongest message ("I Used to Work for the Insurance Company. Now I Work for You.") was a small subheading under a generic keyword headline.
- The attorney photo, name, and bar number appeared only on the About page.
- Interior pages had no reviews, no trust signals, and no closing call to action.
- No "what happens next" explanation and thin objection handling.
- The Mercedes-Benz page was short for the featured lemon brand, and several high-value topics had no page.
- The Spanish home page showed a raw "[CONFIRM: ...]" placeholder to visitors. Spanish and Armenian home pages lacked a text button, process steps, and the attorney photo.
- One broken anchor link on the lemon law FAQ. Photos were served at full size. No Attorney, Article, or WebSite schema.
- About page used puffery ("maximum compensation," "extensive litigation experience"). Rewritten around verifiable facts.

## What I changed

**Conversion and trust**
- Home hero: the insurance tagline is now the dominant headline (keyword H1 kept above it for SEO), with an attorney photo chip showing name, role, and State Bar No. 355861.
- Trust bar on every page: Google rating, former insurance company trial attorney, no fee unless we win, Armenian and Spanish.
- Attorney card under every form. Form button now reads "Get My Free Case Review."
- Three Google reviews (Humberto first) and a closing call, text, and review band on every interior page.

**Home page**
- Six case cards led by high-value matters, attorney photo section, four step process, a lemon law band with Mercedes-Benz first, three "already dealing with an insurer" cards, and eleven FAQs (cost, offers, timeline, fault, immigration status, switching lawyers).

**Lemon law**
- Mercedes-Benz page rebuilt: qualification checklist, EQ, Airmatic, MBUX and electrical, engine, and Sprinter sections, repurchase process, fees paid by the manufacturer, eight FAQs. Mercedes-Benz is first in the Lemon Law menu.

**SEO and speed**
- Schema: LegalService and Attorney with attorney Person, WebSite, Article on guides, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
- Better titles and descriptions on truck, wrongful death, practice areas, and About.
- Smaller image copies with lazy loading. Broken anchor fixed. FAQs are tap-to-open rows.

**Spanish and Armenian**
- Both home pages now match the English structure (buttons, case cards, attorney photo, steps, reviews, FAQs). Placeholder removed.

**Design**
- Refined buttons, spacing, heading accents, cards, two column menu, and footer (bar number, Resources column).
