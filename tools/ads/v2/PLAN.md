# PLG Google Ads v2 Plan

Upload order: 1-campaigns, 2-adgroups, 3-keywords, 4-ads, 5-changes, then paste negatives.txt per campaign.

## Core idea
At $115/day (about 4 clicks at observed CPCs) the firm cannot win generic "car accident lawyer [city]" auctions against six figure advertisers. v2 moves bids to searches that (a) signal a large policy or serious injury, or (b) match the former insurance attorney angle.

## Changes ranked by expected case value
1. Commercial Vehicle (LA, IE; $45, $42): company vehicle, delivery van, Amazon, FedEx, UPS, box and dump trucks. Commercial policies, employer liability. IE is a logistics corridor.
2. Wrongful Death ($45, $40) and Serious Injury ($40, $36): brain, spine, surgery, fractures.
3. Truck and Rideshare: new long tail keywords (big rig, tractor trailer, passenger, delivery app drivers) plus a second Rideshare ad about the 2026 cut to rideshare UM coverage (SB 371, already on the landing page).
4. Mercedes-Benz lemon law: five new ad groups, Buyback ($32), EQ Electric ($30), Models: GLE, GLS, S-Class, AMG, G-Class, 48V battery ($30), Sprinter ($28), Defects ($25).
5. Uninsured Driver ($32, $28) and Insurance Dispute ($30, $26): UM/UIM, lowball offer, denied claim, adjuster, recorded statement.
6. Bus Accident and Hit and Run, Pedestrian (LA, $32 each), Bicycle ($25).
7. Switch Lawyer ($22, $20): already signed cases, low competition.
8. Lemon, Shop Days and Repeat Repairs ($22): "car in shop 30 days", "dealer cant fix my car". Paused brand negatives still apply.
9. Language: Spanish truck, Uber, uninsured group in both injury campaigns ($28, $25), Lemon Spanish ($12), nine added Armenian keywords including transliterated and Armenian script terms.
10. Brand campaign "PLG | Brand", $5/day, $4 bid: protects the firm name and attorney name cheaply. Target California.

## Negatives
About 150 new per injury campaign, 110 for lemon: DIY and info, other practice areas, property damage only, insurer service queries, criminal hit and run, competitor names, other states (lemon). "calculator" is negative for injury (info intent) but allowed for lemon, where it matches the buyback page. LA and Lemon lists contain new terms only. The IE list is complete because no IE negatives file existed.

## Flags for review

- Spanish ads do not claim Spanish speaking staff. Add "Hablamos Español" only if true.

## Watch in 2 to 3 weeks
- Search terms report twice a week. Add negatives, promote converting queries to exact.
- Impression share on Commercial Vehicle, Wrongful Death, Serious Injury, and MB groups. If below 30 percent from rank, raise bids $5 and cut generics further.
- Cost per qualified lead by ad group, not cost per click.
- Insurance Dispute and Switch Lawyer lead quality. Lower bids if calls are property damage or first party disputes.
- If generic city groups spend over 25 percent of injury budget without a signed case, pause Burbank and Pasadena.
