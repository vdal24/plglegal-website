## What to Do After a Pasadena Crash

1. Call 911. Pasadena Police generally handle city streets; CHP handles the freeways.
2. Photograph the vehicles, the scene, and your injuries.
3. Exchange insurance and license information.
4. See a doctor promptly.
5. Do not give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer.
6. Call us.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who takes the police report for a Pasadena crash?
Pasadena Police Department typically handles crashes on city streets. Crashes on the 210, 134, or 110 are generally handled by the California Highway Patrol.

### What if I was hurt in traffic around a Rose Bowl event?
Event traffic, temporary lane closures, and crowds raise crash risk. Your claim works like any other car accident claim, but if a government-controlled road condition contributed, a six-month claim deadline may apply.
