## Where San Gabriel Valley Crashes Happen

- **The 10 freeway (San Bernardino Freeway),** with heavy commuter traffic and frequent rear-end crashes
- **The 60, 605, and 57 freeways,** with big rig traffic heading to and from the Inland Empire and the ports
- **The 210 freeway,** with high speeds along the foothills
- **Busy streets** like Valley Boulevard, Garvey Avenue, Azusa Avenue, and Holt Avenue, with intersection and pedestrian collisions

## What to Do After a Crash

1. Call 911 and get medical help.
2. Photograph the vehicles, the scene, and your injuries.
3. Get the other driver's insurance and license information.
4. See a doctor promptly, even if you feel okay.
5. Do not give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer.
6. Call us.

Full checklist: [What to do after a car accident in California](/guides/what-to-do-after-car-accident-california/)

## Important Deadlines

- **2 years** for most personal injury lawsuits in California
- **3 years** for property damage
- **6 months** to file a government claim if a public agency, city vehicle, or bus was involved

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to drive to Glendale to meet you?
No. We handle San Gabriel Valley cases by phone, text, and video, and we can come to you if you are injured. Most clients never need to visit our office.

### Who takes the police report for a crash in the San Gabriel Valley?
Crashes on freeways like the 10, 210, 605, 57, and 60 are generally handled by the California Highway Patrol. Crashes on city streets are handled by the local police department or the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department, depending on the city.

### Was a city vehicle, bus, or public road involved?
Claims against a public agency generally require a written government claim within six months. Call us right away so that deadline is not missed.
