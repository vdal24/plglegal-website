## Where Antelope Valley Crashes Happen

- **The 14 freeway,** with long daily commutes to the San Fernando and Santa Clarita Valleys, high speeds, and sudden slowdowns
- **Highway 138 (Pearblossom Highway and Avenue D),** two-lane desert roads where head-on and passing collisions can be severe
- **Wind, dust, and night driving** on unlit rural roads
- **Busy streets** like Avenue K, Avenue S, Palmdale Boulevard, and 10th Street West, with intersection and left-turn crashes

## Serious Injuries on High-Speed Roads

Crashes at highway speed often cause fractures, spinal injuries, and head injuries that need long-term care. These cases call for careful documentation of future medical needs and lost income. Learn more about [catastrophic injury cases](/practice-areas/catastrophic-injury/).

## What to Do After a Crash

1. Call 911 and get medical help.
2. Photograph the vehicles, the scene, and your injuries.
3. Get the other driver's insurance and license information.
4. See a doctor promptly, even if you feel okay.
5. Do not give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer.
6. Call us.

Full checklist: [What to do after a car accident in California](/guides/what-to-do-after-car-accident-california/)

## Important Deadlines

- **2 years** for most personal injury lawsuits in California
- **3 years** for property damage
- **6 months** to file a government claim if a public agency, city vehicle, or bus was involved

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to drive to Glendale to meet you?
No. We handle Antelope Valley cases by phone, text, and video, and we can come to you if you are injured. Most clients never need to visit our office.

### Who takes the police report for a crash in the Antelope Valley?
Crashes on the 14 freeway and state highways like the 138 are generally handled by the California Highway Patrol. Crashes on city streets in Lancaster and Palmdale are generally handled by the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department, which serves both cities.

### Was a city vehicle, bus, or public road involved?
Claims against a public agency generally require a written government claim within six months. Call us right away so that deadline is not missed.
