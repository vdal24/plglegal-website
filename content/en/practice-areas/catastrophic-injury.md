## Serious Injuries Need a Different Kind of Claim

Most injury claims are about getting someone back to where they were. A catastrophic injury claim is different. It has to pay for what life looks like from now on: future surgeries, therapy, equipment, help at home, and income you may never be able to earn.

Insurance companies know these are their most expensive claims, and they defend them that way. ## Injuries We Handle

- **Traumatic brain injuries,** including concussions with lasting symptoms
- **Spinal cord injuries** and partial or complete paralysis
- **Neck and back injuries that require surgery,** such as fusions and disc replacements
- **Amputations** and crush injuries
- **Severe burns** and scarring
- **Multiple fractures** and orthopedic injuries that need hardware or repeat surgery
- **Internal organ damage**
- **Loss of vision or hearing**

These injuries most often follow [truck accidents](/practice-areas/truck-accidents/), [commercial vehicle crashes](/practice-areas/commercial-vehicle-accidents/), high-speed [car accidents](/practice-areas/car-accidents/), [motorcycle accidents](/practice-areas/motorcycle-accidents/), and pedestrian collisions. When a family loses someone, see [wrongful death](/practice-areas/wrongful-death/).

## What a Complete Claim Includes

- **Past and future medical care:** hospital bills, surgery, rehabilitation, medication, and the care doctors expect you to need later
- **Home and vehicle changes:** ramps, accessible bathrooms, mobility equipment
- **In-home care,** whether provided by professionals or by family members
- **Lost income and lost earning capacity,** including a career you can no longer pursue
- **Pain, loss of independence, and loss of enjoyment of life**
- **The effect on your spouse and family**

Every case is different, and no lawyer can promise a result. What we can promise is preparation: a claim documented well enough that the insurance company has to take it seriously.

## How We Build a Serious Injury Case {: .sec-steps }

1. **Protect the evidence.** We send preservation letters, obtain reports, video, and vehicle data, and identify every person and company that may be responsible.
2. **Find every insurance policy.** Personal, commercial, employer, umbrella, and your own underinsured motorist coverage. In a serious case, the first policy is rarely the only one.
3. **Document the future.** We work with your treating doctors and, when needed, qualified medical and financial witnesses to show what your care and lost income will cost over time.
4. **Demand full value, and be ready for court.** We handle cases before a lawsuit and in litigation. Insurers pay more attention to a claim that is ready to be tried.

## Do Not Settle Early

Adjusters often contact seriously injured people within days, sometimes with an offer. A release signed in the first weeks cannot be undone when a doctor later recommends surgery. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What counts as a catastrophic injury?
There is no single legal definition. The term generally describes injuries with permanent or long-term effects, such as traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord injuries and paralysis, amputations, severe burns, multiple fractures that require surgery, and loss of vision or hearing.

### How is a catastrophic injury claim valued?
Value depends on the facts. These claims typically include past and future medical care, in-home care and equipment, lost income and lost earning capacity, and compensation for pain, loss of independence, and the effect on family life. Future costs are usually documented with treating doctors and qualified medical and financial witnesses.

### What if the at-fault driver does not have enough insurance?
Serious injuries often exceed a single auto policy. We look for every source of recovery, including employer and commercial policies, umbrella policies, other responsible parties, and your own [underinsured motorist coverage](/practice-areas/uninsured-motorist-claims/).

### How long do I have to file?
Most California injury lawsuits must be filed within two years of the injury. A claim against a city, county, or state agency generally requires a written claim within six months. Different rules can apply to minors. Do not wait to get advice.

### Can you meet me at the hospital or at home?
Yes. We can meet by phone or video, or come to you or your family at the hospital, a rehabilitation facility, or home.
