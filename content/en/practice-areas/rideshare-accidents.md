## Rideshare Accident Claims Got Harder in 2026

Uber and Lyft are everywhere in Glendale, Burbank, Pasadena, and Hollywood. When a rideshare trip ends in a crash, figuring out who pays is more complicated than a normal car accident, and California made it harder.

**Starting January 1, 2026, SB 371 reduced the uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage that Uber and Lyft must carry during rides from $1 million to $60,000 per person and $300,000 per accident.** If you are hurt by an uninsured or underinsured driver while riding in an Uber or Lyft, there may be far less coverage available than before.

That makes finding every possible source of insurance critical.

## Who May Be Responsible

- **The rideshare company's insurance,** depending on the driver's status
- **The rideshare driver's personal insurance**
- **Another at-fault driver** and their insurance
- **Your own auto policy,** including uninsured and underinsured coverage, even if you were a passenger
- **Vehicle manufacturers or government entities,** if a defect or dangerous road contributed

## Why the Driver's App Status Matters

Rideshare coverage changes depending on what the driver was doing:

- **App off:** only the driver's personal policy
- **App on, waiting for a ride request:** limited rideshare company coverage
- **Ride accepted, en route, or passenger on board:** the rideshare company's higher liability coverage generally applies

We obtain trip data and app records to prove which coverage applies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What changed with Uber and Lyft insurance in 2026?
Starting January 1, 2026, California law (SB 371) reduced the uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage Uber and Lyft must carry during rides from $1 million to $60,000 per person and $300,000 per accident.

### Who pays if my Uber driver caused the accident?
When the driver is on an active trip, the rideshare company's liability policy generally applies. Coverage depends on whether the app was on, whether a ride was accepted, and whether a passenger was in the car.

### I was hit by an uninsured driver while riding in a Lyft. What now?
The rideshare company's uninsured motorist coverage may apply, but after 2026 it is much lower. Your own auto policy may provide additional coverage. We look for every available policy.
