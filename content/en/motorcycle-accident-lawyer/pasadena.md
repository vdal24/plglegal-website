## Riding In and Around Pasadena

Pasadena riders share the 210 and 134 with heavy commuter traffic, and many head north through Altadena and La Cañada Flintridge toward the Angeles Crest Highway. The Arroyo Seco Parkway's tight curves and short ramps are especially dangerous for motorcycles. In town, Colorado Boulevard and Lake Avenue traffic brings left-turn and lane-change crashes.

## Common Causes of Motorcycle Crashes

- Drivers turning left in front of an oncoming rider
- Unsafe lane changes and blind-spot collisions on the 210
- Rear-end crashes in stop-and-go traffic
- Car doors opened into a rider's path
- Road hazards like potholes, gravel, and debris

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The insurer says I was partly at fault. Can I still recover?
Yes. California uses pure comparative fault. Even if you share some blame, you can recover damages reduced by your percentage of fault.

### Is lane splitting legal in California?
Yes. California law recognizes lane splitting, and a rider who was lane splitting lawfully is not automatically at fault.
