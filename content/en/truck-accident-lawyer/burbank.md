## Federal Trucking Rules Matter

Interstate truck drivers are subject to federal safety regulations, including hours-of-service limits on how long they can drive without rest. Fatigue, overloaded trailers, and skipped inspections cause serious crashes, and these violations can prove fault.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What federal rules apply to truck drivers?
Interstate commercial truck drivers must follow federal hours-of-service limits on driving time and required rest breaks, and keep electronic logs. Violations can be strong evidence of fault.

### How long do I have to file a truck accident claim?
In California, the deadline for most injury lawsuits is two years from the crash. If a government vehicle was involved, a claim must generally be filed within six months.
