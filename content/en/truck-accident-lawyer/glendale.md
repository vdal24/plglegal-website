## Act Fast: Trucking Evidence Disappears

Trucking companies often have investigators and insurers working the case within hours. Critical evidence can be overwritten or discarded:

- Electronic logging device (ELD) data showing hours driven
- Engine control module and dashcam data
- Driver qualification files, drug and alcohol tests
- Maintenance and inspection records
- Dispatch communications and delivery schedules

We send preservation demands immediately so this evidence is protected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are truck accident cases different from car accidents?
Trucking companies carry larger insurance policies, follow federal safety rules, and often send investigators to the scene within hours. Evidence like driver logs and electronic data can be lost if it is not preserved quickly.

### Can I sue the trucking company, not just the driver?
Often, yes. Employers are generally responsible for their drivers' negligence on the job, and companies can also be liable for poor hiring, training, maintenance, or loading.
