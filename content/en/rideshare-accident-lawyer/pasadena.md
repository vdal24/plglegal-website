## Rideshare Crashes in Pasadena

Old Pasadena's nightlife, Caltech, the Convention Center, and Rose Bowl events bring heavy Uber and Lyft traffic. Drivers stopping suddenly on Colorado Boulevard, making U-turns for pickups, and watching their phones cause frequent accidents.

## Know the 2026 Coverage Change

Since January 1, 2026, SB 371 has cut the uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage Uber and Lyft must carry during rides from $1 million to $60,000 per person and $300,000 per accident. If an uninsured or underinsured driver hits you while you are in a rideshare, the coverage may be much smaller than you expect.
