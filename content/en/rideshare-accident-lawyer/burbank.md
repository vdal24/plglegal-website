## Rideshare Accidents in Burbank

Hollywood Burbank Airport, the studio lots, and downtown Burbank generate constant Uber and Lyft traffic. Pickups and drop-offs on Hollywood Way, Empire Avenue, and San Fernando Boulevard lead to sudden stops, double parking, and distracted driving.

## Know the 2026 Coverage Change

Since January 1, 2026, SB 371 has cut the uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage Uber and Lyft must carry during rides from $1 million to $60,000 per person and $300,000 per accident. If an uninsured or underinsured driver hits your rideshare, finding every other source of coverage matters more than ever.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does it matter whether the rideshare driver had a passenger?
Yes. The coverage available depends on whether the driver was offline, waiting for a ride request, or on the way to or carrying a passenger. App data shows which period applied.

### I was hit by an Uber driver while I was driving. Can I make a claim?
Yes. If the rideshare driver caused the crash, you may have a claim against the driver's personal policy or the rideshare company's coverage, depending on the driver's app status.
