## Rideshare Crashes in Glendale

Uber and Lyft drivers are constantly picking up and dropping off around the Americana at Brand, the Glendale Galleria, Brand Boulevard's restaurants, and Glendale Station. Sudden stops, illegal U-turns, and distracted drivers checking their apps cause a steady stream of rideshare accidents here.

## The 2026 Insurance Change You Need to Know

Since January 1, 2026, California law (SB 371) has reduced the uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage Uber and Lyft must carry during rides, from $1 million down to $60,000 per person and $300,000 per accident. If you are hurt by an uninsured or underinsured driver while riding in a rideshare, the available coverage may be much smaller than it used to be.

That makes it critical to identify every available policy: the rideshare company's, the drivers', and your own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I was a passenger in an Uber in Glendale and got hurt. Who pays?
If your driver caused the crash during your trip, Uber's liability coverage generally applies. If another driver caused it, that driver's insurance is first in line, and rideshare and your own uninsured coverage may help if they are underinsured.

### Do I need to report the accident to Uber or Lyft?
Yes, report it through the app, but keep it brief and factual. Then talk to a lawyer before giving any recorded statement to an insurer.
