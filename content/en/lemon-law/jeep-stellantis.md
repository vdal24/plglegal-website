## Jeep, Ram, Dodge, and Chrysler Owners

Jeep, Ram, Dodge, and Chrysler are all made by Stellantis (FCA US). If your Wrangler, Gladiator, Grand Cherokee, Cherokee, Compass, Wagoneer, Ram 1500, Ram HD, ProMaster, Durango, Charger, Hornet, or Pacifica keeps going back to the dealer, California's lemon law may require the manufacturer to buy it back or replace it, plus pay your attorney fees.

## Problems Owners Commonly Report

Examples of reported issues and recalls:

- **Steering "death wobble"** in solid-front-axle Jeeps, a violent shake at highway speeds that many Wrangler and Gladiator owners have reported
- **4xe plug-in hybrid battery concerns,** including a fire-risk recall in which owners were told to park outside and avoid charging
- **9-speed transmission problems:** harsh shifts, hesitation, and unexpected neutral
- **Electrical and Uconnect failures,** camera blackouts, and battery drain
- **Engine problems,** oil leaks, and coolant loss
- **Water leaks** in removable-top Jeeps

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Jeep "death wobble" covered by lemon law?
It can be. A violent steering shake at highway speed can substantially impair safety. If it returns after repairs during the warranty period, it may support a lemon law claim.

### Are Jeep 4xe plug-in hybrids covered?
Yes. Plug-in hybrid systems, including the high-voltage battery, are covered by the manufacturer's warranty and California's lemon law.

### Who is the manufacturer for Jeep, Ram, Dodge, and Chrysler?
These brands are made by Stellantis, which operates in the U.S. as FCA US LLC. Lemon law claims are made against the manufacturer, not the dealer.
