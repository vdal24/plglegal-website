## Nissan and Infiniti Owners

Nissan's Rogue, Altima, Sentra, Kicks, Versa, Pathfinder, Murano, Frontier, Ariya, and Infiniti models are everywhere in California. Many owners know the feeling of a transmission that shudders, hesitates, or whines. If your vehicle keeps going back for the same warranty defect, California's lemon law may require Nissan to repurchase or replace it and pay your attorney fees.

## Nissan Problems Owners Commonly Report

- **CVT transmission problems:** shuddering, hesitation, jerking, whining, overheating, and loss of power. Nissan's CVTs have been the subject of numerous complaints, class actions, and extended warranty programs.
- **Automatic emergency braking activating without reason**
- **Electrical and infotainment failures**
- **Ariya EV charging and range concerns**
- **Air conditioning failures**
- **Engine and oil leaks**

## An Extended Warranty Is Not a Fix

Nissan has extended warranty coverage on some CVTs. More coverage means more trips to the dealer, not a vehicle that works. If the transmission problem keeps returning, California's lemon law may entitle you to a buyback rather than another replacement transmission.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Nissan CVT problem make my car a lemon?
Yes, if the CVT problem substantially impairs the vehicle and the dealer could not fix it after a reasonable number of attempts during the warranty period.

### Nissan extended my CVT warranty. Do I still have lemon law rights?
An extended warranty does not erase your rights. If the transmission problem keeps returning, you may still be entitled to a buyback. Talk to a lawyer before signing any release.

### Is Infiniti covered?
Yes. Infiniti vehicles sold or leased in California with a new vehicle warranty are covered.
