After a car accident in Glendale, the steps you take in the first hours and days can significantly impact your ability to recover compensation. California personal injury law provides strong protections for accident victims, but insurance companies work quickly to minimize payouts.

## Seek Medical Attention First

Your health is the priority. Even if you feel fine, some injuries, like whiplash and internal bleeding, may not show symptoms immediately. Document all medical visits and keep records of every treatment.

## Document Everything

Take photos of vehicle damage, the accident scene, road conditions, and any visible injuries. Obtain contact information from witnesses. Request a copy of the police report.

## Do Not Speak with Insurance Adjusters Alone

Insurance adjusters are trained to get you to say things that hurt your claim. Before giving any recorded statement or accepting any settlement offer, consult with a personal injury attorney.

## Know Your Deadlines

California’s statute of limitations for personal injury claims is generally two years from the date of the accident. Missing this deadline can permanently bar your claim.
