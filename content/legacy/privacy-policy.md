**Last Updated:** January 2025

Prospect Law Group ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes
how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit [plglegal.com](https://plglegal.com)
or contact us through our website.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website:

* **Contact form submissions:** name, email address, phone number, and message content.
* **Connect Center submissions:** information you provide through our Connect Center widget, including details about an incident, phone number, and email address.
* **Email and phone inquiries:** information you provide when you email or call our office.
* **Technical data:** browser type, device information, and general usage data that may be collected through cookies or analytics tools if implemented in the future.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

* Respond to your inquiries and evaluate potential legal matters;
* Communicate with you about our services;
* Improve our website and user experience;
* Maintain security and prevent misuse of our website.

## No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties for
their own marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who assist us in
operating our website or communicating with you, subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## Attorney-Client Relationship

Submitting information through this website, including contact forms and the Connect Center,
does not create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is formed only
when we agree to represent you in writing.

## Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality. If we add analytics
tools in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how you can
manage your preferences. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website. However, no
internet transmission is completely secure. Please do not send confidential or sensitive information
until an attorney-client relationship has been established.
