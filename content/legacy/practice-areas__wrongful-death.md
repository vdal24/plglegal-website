## Overview

Losing a loved one because of someone else's negligence is devastating. Families are suddenly faced with funeral expenses, lost financial support, unanswered questions about what happened, and grief that no legal outcome can fully heal. California wrongful death law allows certain family members to pursue accountability and compensation when a preventable accident, medical negligence, workplace incident, or act of violence takes a life.

Prospect Law Group provides compassionate yet aggressive representation for families in Glendale and Los Angeles County. We investigate the circumstances thoroughly, preserving evidence, identifying every liable party, and calculating the full economic and emotional impact on spouses, children, and dependents. Wrongful death claims may arise from car accidents, truck crashes, premises accidents, medical errors, and other negligent conduct.

While no amount of money replaces your loved one, a successful claim can secure stability for funeral and burial costs, lost income and benefits, loss of household services, loss of companionship, and the pain of survivors. We handle these cases with sensitivity and determination so your family can focus on healing while we pursue justice on your behalf.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can file a wrongful death claim in California?

Spouses, children, parents of deceased children, and certain other dependents may file. We evaluate who has standing and whether multiple survivors should participate.

### What is the statute of limitations for wrongful death?

California wrongful death lawsuits generally must be filed within two years of the date of death. Some circumstances may affect deadlines - consult an attorney promptly.

### What damages are available in a wrongful death case?

Funeral expenses, lost financial support, loss of companionship, loss of household services, and certain medical bills before death may be recoverable depending on who brings the claim.

### Can we file if there is also a criminal case?

Yes. Criminal prosecution is separate from civil wrongful death claims. A conviction can help, but civil cases can proceed regardless of criminal outcomes.

### How long do wrongful death cases take?

Timelines vary. Some cases settle in months when liability is clear; complex cases involving multiple defendants or trials may take years. We move efficiently while maximizing recovery.

### Do we need an autopsy for a wrongful death claim?

Not always, but autopsy results can clarify cause of death in disputed cases. We advise families on medical and legal considerations with sensitivity.
