## Frequently Asked Questions - Montrose

### Is your office near Montrose?

Yes. Our Glendale office is roughly 10 minutes from Montrose via the 210 freeway, with free parking available downtown.

### I slipped in a Montrose store - can I sue?

If a business failed to fix a hazard or warn customers, you may have a premises liability claim. Document the scene, report the incident to management, and seek medical care promptly.

### What compensation can I recover for a Montrose accident?

Depending on your case, damages may include medical bills, future treatment, lost wages, pain and suffering, and property damage. We evaluate all categories during your consultation.

### The other driver was ticketed - does that guarantee I win?

A traffic citation helps establish fault but does not automatically set damages. Insurers may still fight injury severity - we document your full harm.

### Do you handle dog bite cases in Montrose?

Yes. California strict liability often applies to dog bite injuries. We pursue compensation from homeowners or renters insurance when applicable.
