**Last updated:** January 2025

Prospect Law Group is committed to making our website accessible to as many people as possible,
including individuals with disabilities. We aim to follow widely recognized accessibility practices,
including guidance aligned with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA,
where practicable.

## Our approach

Rather than relying on third-party overlay widgets, we build accessibility into the site itself.
This includes semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, visible focus indicators,
descriptive labels, text alternatives for meaningful images, and support for reduced motion
preferences.

## Ongoing efforts

We regularly review our website and work to improve usability and accessibility.
Some areas may still be in progress as content and features are updated.

## No guarantee of compliance

While we strive to maintain an accessible website, we do not guarantee that every page or
feature will meet all accessibility standards at all times. This statement is not a claim of
full ADA compliance or guaranteed WCAG conformance.

## Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you need information
in an alternative format, please contact us. We will work with you to provide the information
or service through a method that works for you.
