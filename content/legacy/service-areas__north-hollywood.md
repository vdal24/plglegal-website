## Frequently Asked Questions - North Hollywood

### Is North Hollywood within your service area?

Yes. We serve North Hollywood and all of Los Angeles County. Where you live does not limit our ability to represent you.

### I was injured on Lankershim Boulevard - what should I do first?

Get medical treatment, file a police report if applicable, photograph the scene, and avoid signing anything from insurers. Then contact us for a free case review.

### Can you handle accidents on the 170 near North Hollywood?

Yes. Freeway merge and exit ramp accidents on the 170 and nearby connectors are common cases we pursue against negligent drivers and carriers.

### What if I was partially at fault for my NoHo accident?

California comparative fault may still allow recovery if another party shares responsibility. We analyze police reports and evidence to protect your percentage of fault.

### How much does a North Hollywood personal injury lawyer cost?

We work on contingency - no upfront attorney fee. Our fee is a percentage of recovery only if we win compensation for you. The consultation is free.
