Engine Problems and California Lemon Law

Engine stalling, losing power, burning oil, or check engine light that keeps coming back? Your car may qualify as a lemon in California. Free case review: 323-452-5222.

When Your Engine Cannot Be Trusted

An engine that stalls in traffic, loses power on the freeway, or burns through oil is more than an inconvenience. Engine defects can substantially impair your vehicle's use, value, and safety, which is what California's lemon law is designed to address.

Engine Defects That Often Support Lemon Law Claims

  • Stalling or shutting off while driving
  • Loss of power or reduced power mode
  • Excessive oil consumption or oil leaks
  • Overheating and coolant loss
  • Knocking, ticking, or misfires
  • Rough idle and hard starts
  • Check engine lights that keep returning
  • Engine failure requiring replacement

Recalls Involving Engines

Several manufacturers have issued major engine recalls in recent years, including certain GM 6.2L V8 trucks and SUVs, Toyota Tundra and Lexus LX twin-turbo V6 models, and Hyundai and Kia models with Theta II engines. A recall repair that does not solve the problem, or that keeps your vehicle out of service for weeks, can be strong evidence in a lemon law case.

Safety Defects Need Fewer Repair Attempts

California's lemon law presumption is stronger for serious safety defects. If a defect likely to cause death or serious injury, like stalling on the freeway, was subject to repair two or more times within 18 months or 18,000 miles, the vehicle is presumed to be a lemon.

What to Do

  • Pull over safely and record what happened, including any warning messages
  • Have the car towed if it is unsafe, and keep the towing receipt
  • Make sure the repair order describes the symptom accurately

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a check engine light enough for a lemon law claim?

It can be, if the light keeps returning for the same underlying defect and the dealer cannot fix it. The key is the defect behind the light and whether it substantially impairs the vehicle.

My engine was replaced under warranty. Can I still pursue a lemon law claim?

Yes. An engine replacement is a repair attempt. Depending on your history, it may support a claim, especially if problems continue or the car was out of service for a long time.

Is stalling considered a safety defect?

Stalling while driving can be a defect likely to cause death or serious injury. Under California's presumption, two repair attempts for such a defect within 18 months or 18,000 miles can be enough.
