The California Car Accident Claim Process, Step by Step

How a California car accident claim works from the first call to settlement or trial, and how long each stage usually takes. Free consultation: 323-452-5222.

Call 323-452-5222Free Case Review

Step 1: Free Consultation (Day 1)

We learn what happened, review your injuries and insurance, and explain your options. If we take your case, you pay nothing up front.

Step 2: Investigation (First Weeks)

We send letters of representation to all insurers so they contact us, not you. We gather the police report, photos, video, witness statements, and vehicle damage records. For serious crashes, we may preserve vehicle data or hire an accident reconstruction expert.

Step 3: Medical Treatment (Weeks to Months)

Your job is to get better. We help coordinate care if needed, including providers who can treat you on a lien when you do not have health insurance. We track your bills and records.

Step 4: Demand Package (After Treatment or a Clear Prognosis)

When your treatment ends or your doctors can project future care, we prepare a demand package: medical records and bills, wage loss documentation, photos, and a detailed explanation of how the injury has affected your life.

Step 5: Negotiation (Weeks to Months)

The insurer responds, usually with a low offer. We negotiate with the evidence. Many claims resolve here.

Step 6: Lawsuit, If Needed

If the insurer will not be fair, we file suit before the two-year deadline. Litigation includes written discovery, depositions, medical examinations, and usually mediation.

Step 7: Settlement or Trial

Most cases settle before trial. If yours does not, we are prepared to try it.

Step 8: Getting Paid

Settlement funds go into our client trust account. We resolve medical liens and bills, often negotiating them down, and you receive your share.

Typical Timeline

Stage Common timeframe
Investigation First few weeks
Medical treatment A few weeks to a year or more
Demand and negotiation 1 to 4 months after treatment
Litigation, if needed 1 to 2+ years

Every case is different. We keep you updated at every stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a car accident settlement take in California?

Many claims settle within several months to a year after medical treatment ends. Cases that go to litigation commonly take one to two years or more.

Why do you wait until treatment is finished to settle?

You only get one settlement. Waiting until your doctors know the full extent of your injuries, or can project future care, prevents settling for less than your claim is worth.

Will my case go to trial?

Most cases settle. We prepare every case as if it will go to trial because that preparation is what produces fair settlements.
